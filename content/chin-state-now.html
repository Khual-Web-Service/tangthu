Chin State Today: Life in the Chin Hills in 2026

Chin State, home of the Zomi, Chin, Lai and related peoples, is going through some of its hardest years. Families have been displaced, roads are cut and prices have climbed. [[un-mm-displacement-2026]] [[cg-tedim-prices]] Classrooms and clinics are reopening all the same, and communities abroad keep their culture alive and send help home. Updated November 2026. The newest figures we could verify date from August 2026, so the situation may have changed. Independent reporting inside Chin State is very limited. Where sources disagree, we say so. For the history behind today, see Modern History.

The land and its people {{doc}}

Chin State had 478,801 people in the 2014 census, the smallest population of any state or region in Myanmar. [[nation-census2015]] Its highest mountain, Nat Ma Taung (Mount Victoria), is the heart of an ASEAN Heritage Park known for its rhododendrons and orchids, and for the white-browed nuthatch, a bird found nowhere else. [[acb-natmataung]]

Even before the 2021 coup, Chin State had Myanmar’s highest poverty rate: almost six in ten people were poor in 2017. [[undp-poverty2019]]

How many people have been displaced? {{doc}}

The UN counted 117,900 internally displaced people in Chin State as of 10 August 2026, nearly all of them displaced after February 2021. [[un-mm-displacement-2026]] A UN estimate cited by DVB puts the displaced share at about one in five residents. [[dvb-cnf-malaysia2025]]

Township figures come from local groups and are estimates. More than 20,000 people were reported displaced in Falam township in late 2025, and about 17,000 in Mindat township in mid-2026. [[bni-falam2025]] [[cg-mindat2026]] About 500 people returned to Falam in May 2026, after the military retook the town. [[cg-falam-return]]

Across the border, Mizoram’s government counts 28,964 refugees from Myanmar in 11 districts. Aid agencies estimate the real number is 50,000 to 60,000, because many people are not registered. [[organiser-mizoram2026]] India brought in tighter movement rules in June 2026, and some families were left waiting near the border. [[ocha-hu52]]

Daily life: prices, health and connection {{doc}}

Nationally, the UN says 16.2 million people need aid, and only 43% of its 2026 appeal had been funded by mid-year. Fighting around Falam disrupted the supply of food and medicine. [[ocha-hu52]] WFP names Chin among the areas hit hardest by rising fuel costs. [[wfp-2026]]

In Tedim, checkpoint fees on the road from Kalay were reported to have doubled the price of goods such as rice and fuel. [[cg-tedim-prices]] Tedim’s 100-bed hospital lost its specialist staff, and patients now travel to Tio on the Mizoram border or to Kalay for care. [[cg-tedim-health]] In June 2026, a landslide blocked the Gangaw–Rezua road, an important route for supplies and aid. [[cg-landslide2026]]

According to local residents, internet service is cut across the state, and many people rely on satellite connections. [[bni-starlink2026]]

Who controls which towns? {{doc}}

Control changed again in 2026. The military retook Falam in April and Tonzang in May. [[dvb-falam2026]] [[ap-tonzang2026]] A July 2026 summary listed Hakha, Falam, Thantlang, Tedim and Tonzang as military-held; Mindat, Kanpetlet, Matupi, Cikha and Rihkhawdar as held by Chin resistance groups; and Paletwa and Samee as held by the Arakan Army. [[bni-control2026]]

Reports do not all agree. Al Jazeera reported that by late May the military claimed control of the whole northern part of the state, which conflicts with the July summary for some northern towns. [[aljazeera2026]] AP noted that independent confirmation was virtually impossible. [[ap-tonzang2026]] Monitors cited by Al Jazeera recorded more airstrikes in Chin from April to June 2026 than in any other state. [[aljazeera2026]]

In the December 2025 election, voting in Chin State took place only in a few military-held towns. [[mnow-election2026]] In February 2025, rival Chin bodies agreed in Aizawl to merge into one Chin National Council. [[dvb-cnc2025]] In April 2026, DVB reported no public progress on the merger. [[dvb-falam2026]]

Schools, clinics and young people {{doc}}

In June 2026, nearly 3,000 students in Falam township sat placement exams at eight centres after a three-year gap in schooling. [[cg-falam-exams]] More than 23,000 students enrolled in Hakha, Falam, Tedim and Tonzang, while schools in Mindat, Matupi, Paletwa, Thantlang and Kanpetlet remained closed. [[cg-enrollment2026]]

In March 2026, a new clinic opened in Chakhai village in the remote Mara area of Matupi township. [[cg-mara-clinic]] In May 2026, young people from across the Chin community met in Aizawl and agreed to form a Chin Youth Congress. [[cg-youth2026]] Some farmers are turning to crops such as elephant foot yam as a lasting source of income. [[ebo-yam2025]]

The Zomi and Chin abroad {{doc}}

Hundreds gathered in Indianapolis for the 78th Chin National Day in February 2026. The local Chin community is estimated at about 35,000. [[mirrorindy-cnd2026]] Chin churches in the United States have raised more than $10 million for causes at home. [[aljazeera-diaspora2023]] In October 2025, communities in Macau, Aizawl, Malaysia, Australia and the US celebrated the harvest New Year, called Khuado in Tedim and Tonzang.
