Contribute

This archive grows through the community. Photographs, recordings, family genealogies and corrections are all welcome.

What we are looking for

  • Photographs — historic or recent, with date, place, people (with consent) and photographer.
  • Oral histories — audio or written accounts from elders. Please note speaker, village, clan, dialect and date of recording.
  • Corrections & sources — if a statement is wrong or incomplete, send a citable source.

Consent and care

  • We publish only with the speaker’s informed consent (or a family member’s for deceased elders).
  • Testimonies about conflict or displacement can be anonymised; we will never publish location details that could put someone at risk.
  • Oral histories are labelled {{oral}} and are not edited to fit written records.

How to send

Email tangthu@khual.net

No account is needed. To help us check your contribution, please include:

  • A correction: the page, the sentence, and a source.
  • An oral history: the speaker’s name, village, clan, dialect and date, and their agreement to publication.
  • A photo: the date, the place, who is in it, and who took it.

Developers can also open an issue or pull request on GitHub.
