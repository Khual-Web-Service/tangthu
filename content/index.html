Zomi and Zo history: a shared story across three borders

Across Northeast India, Myanmar and Bangladesh, the Chin, Kuki and Mizo peoples share an origin story that predates the borders now dividing them. [[haokip2026]] This site brings together what oral tradition remembers and what the written record documents — and keeps the two clearly labelled.

  1. Long agoAncestors come out of a cave called Khul, or Chhinlung. The story ties Zo families together across today’s borders. [[haokip2026]]
  2. Before 1400People live in the Kabaw and Kale valleys of Myanmar, then move up into the hills. Villages like Ciimnuai grow near Tedim. [[lehman-inscr]] [[carey1896v2]]
  3. 1890sBritish armies take the hills and divide them between Burma and India. Those lines are still the borders today. [[wiki-chinrising]] [[pau2019]]
  4. 1899–1902Missionaries arrive in Hakha, and Pau Cin Hau creates a Zo script of his own. Reading and writing begin to spread. [[lian2021]] [[pandey-roadmap]]
  5. 1947–1948Chin leaders help found Burma at Panglong, then vote for democracy on 20 February, now Chin National Day. [[iseas-panglong]] [[chankhar]]
  6. TodayWar and hard times scatter families from Mizoram to Malaysia, Indianapolis and Australia. The story keeps going, and it includes you. [[asianews]] [[indy]]

Zogam: where the Zo live

Zo nationalist writers describe the Zo homeland as spanning Northeast India (Mizoram, Manipur, Tripura), western Myanmar (Chin State) and Bangladesh’s Chittagong Hill Tracts. Questions about Zomi and Zo history

What is Zomi history?

The history of the peoples who call themselves Zomi (“Zo people”), including Tedim, Paite, Zou, Simte, Vaiphei and related communities, in Chin State, Manipur, Mizoram and the diaspora. The name was proposed in 1950 and adopted by the Zomi Baptist Convention in 1953. [[thangsuanhau]] See Names & Identity.

What is Zo history?

The history of the wider family of related peoples, Zomi, Chin, Kuki, Lai, Mizo and Zou, who share Kuki-Chin languages [[vanbik2009]] and origin traditions such as Khul and Chhinlung. [[haokip2026]]

Where did the Zo and Zomi people come from?

Oral tradition tells of emergence from a cave called Khul or Chhinlung. Historians trace movement through the Kabaw and Kale valleys into the Chin Hills, and Burmese inscriptions mention the Chin by the 12th century. [[lehman-inscr]] See Origins & Migration.

What is the difference between Zo, Zomi, Chin, Kuki and Mizo?

The names overlap. Zomi and Mizo both mean “Zo people.” In 1896 British officers recorded that the Northern Chins called themselves Yo, the Haka and southern tribes Lai, and the Chins of Lower Burma Shu. [[carey1896]]

What is Zogam?

“The land of the Zo”: the hill country of Chin State in Myanmar, Manipur and Mizoram in India, and the Chittagong Hill Tracts in Bangladesh. [[wiki-zonat]]
