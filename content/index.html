The story in brief

The Zomi, Zo, Chin, Kuki, Lai, Mizo and Zou peoples share languages and origin traditions. [[vanbik2009]] Their homeland spans the hills of Chin State in Myanmar, Manipur and Mizoram in India, and the Chittagong Hill Tracts in Bangladesh. [[wiki-zonat]] Oral tradition places their ancestors’ emergence at Khul or Chhinlung, and historians trace movement through the Kabaw and Kale valleys into the Chin Hills. [[haokip2026]] [[lehman-inscr]] In the 1890s British annexation divided the hills between Burma, Assam and Bengal, and the Zou Gal of 1917 to 1919 followed. [[wiki-chinrising]] [[guite2019]] Missionaries reached Hakha in 1899, and Pau Cin Hau created a script. [[lian2021]] [[pandey-roadmap]] Panglong in 1947 and independence in 1948 shaped the modern states. [[iseas-panglong]] Chin National Day has been marked on 20 February since 1948. [[bni-cnd]] The Zomi name was adopted in 1953. [[thangsuanhau]] Pau Cin Hau’s script entered Unicode in 2014. [[unicode7]] Families have since settled far away, in Indianapolis and in Malaysia. [[indy]] [[unhcr2019]] Which name a community prefers is debated, and each view is set out side by side on Names & Identity.

Common questions

What is Zomi history?

The history of the peoples who call themselves Zomi (“Zo people”), including Tedim, Paite, Zou, Simte, Vaiphei and related communities, in Chin State, Manipur, Mizoram and the diaspora. The name was proposed in 1950 and adopted by the Zomi Baptist Convention in 1953. [[thangsuanhau]] See Names & Identity.

What is Zo history?

The history of the wider family of related peoples, Zomi, Chin, Kuki, Lai, Mizo and Zou, who share Kuki-Chin languages [[vanbik2009]] and origin traditions such as Khul and Chhinlung. [[haokip2026]]

Where did the Zo and Zomi people come from?

Oral tradition tells of emergence from a cave called Khul or Chhinlung. Historians trace movement through the Kabaw and Kale valleys into the Chin Hills, and Burmese inscriptions mention the Chin by the 12th century. [[lehman-inscr]] See Origins & Migration.

What is the difference between Zo, Zomi, Chin, Kuki and Mizo?

The names overlap. Zomi and Mizo both mean “Zo people.” In 1896 British officers recorded that the Northern Chins called themselves Yo, the Haka and southern tribes Lai, and the Chins of Lower Burma Shu. [[carey1896]]

What is Zogam?

“The land of the Zo”: the hill country of Chin State in Myanmar, Manipur and Mizoram in India, and the Chittagong Hill Tracts in Bangladesh. [[wiki-zonat]]
